# The Dark Sun over Europe: the men of the Democratic Quartet.
#
# Sweden after Germany's Totaler Sieg (common/national_focus/wif_swe_dark_sun.txt).
# The four democratic parties hold the Riksdag together as a Democratic Unity
# Government while Hitler's Trio works on the country from the right - see
# common/bop/wif_swe_soul_of_the_fatherland.txt.
#
# NONE OF THESE IS RECRUITED AT GAME START. SWE_the_costs_of_the_war recruits
# them (recruit_character works at runtime as well as in history). Their
# country_leader roles are therefore inert until then and cannot take the
# Swedish leadership away from Per Albin Hansson in 1936.
#
# Tage Erlander already exists in vanilla's common/characters/SWE.txt and is
# recruited at game start, so a character id cannot be redefined here: his
# advisor role is added to his entry in SWE.txt, and his country leader role
# is added by the focus at runtime (a static one would compete with Hansson for
# the socialist leadership from day one).
#
# THE LEADER IS NEVER HIS OWN ADVISOR. Every advisor role here carries
# is_country_leader = no, as vanilla's Pehrsson-Bramstorp does.
