# Blank focus tree for every Moskowien civil war breakaway, and for the Far
# Eastern Union.
#
# The FES is not a rump state - it is Stalin's government in exile with 40% of
# the Red Army - but it has no tree of its own yet and the generic one would be
# worse than nothing. Blank for now; give it a real tree and remove the tag.
#
# The Siberian Anarchy is blank for the opposite reason. It is not waiting for
# content: a country with no government should not be offered national focuses
# at all, so this one is permanent.
#
# The Central Siberian Republic, the West Siberian Marxist Republic and the
# Central Asian Red Army are here on the same terms as the FES - placeholders
# until they have trees of their own.
#
# NAR and NWG are deliberately NOT in this list. They are Moskowien's loyalist
# emergency governments rather than breakaways, and they still fall through to
# the vanilla generic tree. That is a gap, not a decision - add them here if
# that is wanted.
#
# Add a new tag here the moment it is created, rather than leaving it to fall
# through to the generic tree.
#
# Without this they would fall through to the generic tree, which is full of
# industry and army focuses no rump state fighting for its life should have.
#
# The tree carries a single focus hidden behind allow_branch = { always = no }
