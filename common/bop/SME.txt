# The Soviet Emergency Military Government's balance of power, created by the
# focus SME_address_fracturing_leadership.
#
# The question the focus asks is who the Emergency Government actually is: the
# marshal who holds the army, or the council that is supposed to hold the
# marshal. Nothing in the SME's founding ever settled it, and Popov walking east
# with an army group is what made the question urgent.
#
# ORIENTATION. The council is the LEFT side and runs negative; Zhukov is the
# RIGHT side and runs positive, the same way vanilla puts Italy's Grand Council
# left and Mussolini right. Getting this backwards silently swaps every modifier
# in the file, so if the two ever need to trade places, move the icons and the
# localisation as well as the ranges.
#
# FOUR STEPS EACH WAY, plus a dead centre that pays nothing. The bands are not
# even widths: the first step off centre is the widest at 0.20, and they narrow
# to 0.20 again at the extreme, so drifting slightly is easy and going all the
# way is not.
#
# There is deliberately NO decision_category. Nothing moves this balance yet -
# it is created by the focus and then sits where it was put until an event, a
# focus or a decision pushes it with add_power_balance_value. That is the next
# piece of work, not an oversight; a commented-out category in vanilla's own
# _test.txt confirms the field is optional.
