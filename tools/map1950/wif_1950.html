How this map was made

Start from the 1936 map, then apply in order: the Anglo-German peace map, the Reichskommissariate, the Soviet surrender and the Central Asian breakup, Popov's, Stalin's and Yagoda's Siberia, Japan's dictated peace in China and America out of Asia. Choices where the mod branches: Italy fought, Germany stayed fascist, the Reichskommissariate exist, and neither the German nor the SME collapse has happened yet.
